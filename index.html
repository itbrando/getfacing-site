Why it happens

Two systems decide what shoppers see

The answer comes from the web

AI writes its recommendation from reviews, forums and brand pages. That's how your product gets named.

The buy button comes from feeds

In our data the chosen seller nearly always comes through a merchant product feed, and the cheapest offer wins only about half the time.

Agents read structured data

In our lab tests, an AI stopped picking a leading product once its specs and weight were removed from the product data.
