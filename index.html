FAQ

Questions marketers ask us

Which AI assistants do you track?

ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Grok today, in the real consumer apps. Google AI Mode, Microsoft Copilot and buying agents such as Meta Muse, OpenAI dots and Instinct are coming next.

How is this different from SEO tools?

SEO tools rank links on a results page. AI assistants write one answer and pick a few products. We measure that answer: who is named, recommended, shown for sale and bought.

How do you measure revenue?

We read AI-referred visits and orders from your store's own data, by assistant, and line them up with what each AI showed customers that day.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. We're building multi-brand workspaces so agencies can track and report on every client from one account.

What does it cost?

Plans depend on the brands, products, markets and assistants you track. Book a demo and we'll scope it with you.

Do you change anything in my store?

No. Store connections are read-only.
