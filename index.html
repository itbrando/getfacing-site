How agents shop

Three ways agents reach your products

What we've learned from our benchmarks and evals.

Answer engines

ChatGPT · Perplexity · Gemini · Google AI Mode

Question→Your pages→Pick

They read your pages and what others write about you, then recommend. Most don't run your site's scripts, so details that load late or live in images never reach them.

Where it breaks: details only in images or scripts, and review sites that favor a competitor.

Buying agents

OpenAI dots · Meta Muse · Grok

Task→Your store→Checkout

They drive a real browser on a computer in the cloud and click through to checkout. A few identify themselves; most don't, and bot protection often stops them first. We show which ones your rules stop, so you can decide.

Where it breaks: bot checks, sign-in walls, add-to-cart scripts and free-shipping minimums.

Agentic checkout

Checkout inside ChatGPT and other AI apps (ACP, UCP)

Request→Your catalog→Order

They order through your platform's catalog and checkout, and never see your storefront. Your product data is all they have.

Where it breaks: sizes, options and product codes missing from your data.
