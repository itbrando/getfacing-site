What we've measured

We see what shoppers see

We ask the AI apps the questions real shoppers type, in the consumer apps, with a fresh chat and no history, and record everything a shopper sees. We also test, in a lab, what changes an AI's pick.

6%

The stores the consumer apps sent shoppers to, and the ones their developer APIs cited for the same questions, overlapped by only 6%. You have to measure the real apps.

13 → 0

In our lab, an AI picked a leading tent 13 times in 17. With its weight and specs removed from the product data, it never did.

76 of 79

ChatGPT's top store came through a product catalog feed in 76 of 79 seller lists. It was the cheapest store only about half the time.

Facing.ai panel: 200 answers in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Grok across outdoor gear and beauty, September 30 to October 2, 2026. Lab: 400 controlled trials. Early data, updated as the panel grows.
