What we do

When an agent gets your product wrong, the sale goes somewhere else.

Facing AI watches the agents your customers use, so your team can see this new channel and run it.

  • Monitor

    See what every agent shows

    We continuously check what ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Meta’s Muse and other agents show for your products: the product, size, price, stock and seller, and whether you appear at all.

  • Fix

    Know what to change

    When an agent gets a product wrong, we show why and what to change in the product pages, catalogs and listings you control, then confirm the fix holds.

  • Measure

    Track the channel

    Analytics on how agents present your products over time, by agent and product, so you can see a channel your current reports miss.
